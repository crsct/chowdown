ingredients:
- 40g Oat Flakes (ground)
- 200g Wholewheat flour
- 1 pinch salt
- 1.5 tsp baking powder
- 1/3 tsp baking soda
- 65g brown sugar
- 80ml melted oil (coconut, olive, avocado, ..)
- 240ml milk (almond, oat, soy)
- 200g fresh cherries
- 60g dark chocolate

directions:
- Combine dry ingredients in a mixing bowl
- add milk and oil to it mixing well
- fold in cherries and 3/4 of the chocolate
- spoon the mix into muffin tins
- top with the leftover chocolate
- let bake for 23-25 min at 180-190C
