tags:
- Snack

ingredients:
- Sencha Tea
- 100g Sugar
- Vanilla Pod
- 1 large Pineapple

directions:
- Prepare 700ml of nearly boiling water
- add the sencha inside of a bag (teabag, teaegg)
- add the seeds and pod of vanilla
- let steap and boil for 10 minutes
- preheat the oven 180C
- remove the nescha
- add the sugar
- peel the pineapple and remove its bottom and top
- remove the inner stem
- cut in appropriate form (spears, rings)
- place pineapple in a bakepan and cover with the syrup
- leave in the oven for an hour
- optionally cut the pineapple in more bitesize pieces and roast for 10 more minutes
