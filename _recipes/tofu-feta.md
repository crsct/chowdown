title:  "Tofu Feta"
image: tofu-feta.jpg

tags:
- side

ingredients:
- Olive Oil
- Apple Cidre Vinegar
- Firm Tofu
- Oregano / Herbs of Provence
- Lemon Juice
- Miso Paste
- Yeast Flakes
- Garlic

directions:
- Cut or rip tofu into your favorite size
- boil in salty water
- add liquid ingredients to a container
- mix in miso and garlic
- season to your personal taste
- add tofu and let marinade for some hours before eating
