ingredients:
- 250g Margarine
- 250g Flour
- 175g Sugar
- 4 egg
- 1/8l Redwine
- 1 Vanilasugar
- 1 Backingpowder
- 1tsp Cinamon
- 100g Dark Chocolate
- 2 tbsp Cocoa
- some nelke
- some muskat
- some anis


directions:
- Mix margarine and the eggs
- add the sugars and combine well
- add the redwine
- mix in the backingpowder and flour
- add chocolate, cocoa and the spices
- bake for 60 min on 200deg
