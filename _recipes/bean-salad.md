title: "Bean Salad"
image: bean-salad.jpg

tags:
- summer

ingredients:
- 1 can beans (white, kidney or whatever)
- 1 cucumber
- 2 tomatoes
- lime/lemon juice
- a good hand of mint leaves
- 1 medium red onion
- olive oil

directions:
- cut the cucumber into smallish pieces
- finely dice the onion
- finely chop the mint leaves
- add all to a bowl
- season to taste with pepper, salt and maybe chilli flakes
- add lemon or lime juice to taste
- drizzle with olive oil until consistency of your liking is reached
- garnish with leftover leaves
