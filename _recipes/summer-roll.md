ingredients:
- Cucumber
- Carrots
- Veg of your choice
- Mint
- Basil (Thai)
- Rice Vermicelli Noods
- Rice Paper Wraps
- Sesame Seed for garnish
- Smoked Tofu

directions:
- Julienne Cucumber, Carrots and the tofu
- cut the rest of your veg in an appropriate form 
- cook the noodles to the packaging instructions
- wash off after cooking with cold water to stop overcooking
- wet the wrapper by dragging slowly through a warm water bath
- place the ingredients pointing in one direction in the lower half of the wrap
- pull up the sides and roll over wrapping it gently but toughtly

---

Summery fresh and eaten cold, with minimum cooking required
