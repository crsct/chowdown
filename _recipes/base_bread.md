title:  "Basic Bread"

tags:
- bread

ingredients:
- 500g Flour (Mixed, Dinkel, Weizen)
- 450g Water
- 2 pcs Dry Yeast
- 20g salt
- 20g Lineseed
- 150g Seeds (Pumpkin, Sunflower)
- 50g Seeds to top
- some oil (have to look it up)

directions:
- Add yeast to a portion of the water warm NOT HOT
- Combine dry ingredients
- mix and comibne with the water and yeast mixture
- add the oil
- put into a form
- bake at 200°C Ober Unter without preheating
