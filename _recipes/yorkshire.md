---

title:  "Yorkshire Pudding"
image: yorkshire-pudding.jpg

tags:
- sidedish

ingredients:
- 4 lg eggs
- 125g + 2 tbsp flour
- 250 ml milk
- pinch salt
- 50g butter

directions:
- Add dry ingredients
- mix in the milk and eggs until smooth and lump free
- let it rest for 2 hours
- preheat the oven
- add a tsp or two in each tin and let the tin preheat for 5 to 7 min
- fill each cup to 1/2 to 3/4
- bake to 11 to 13 minutes
- serve immediately
