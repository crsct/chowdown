---

layout: recipe
title:  "Mushroom Sauce"

tags:
- main

ingredients:
- 65g Butter
- 500g Mushrooms
- onion
- 50-70 ml red wine
- thyme
- bayleaf
- 500ml broth
- starch

directions:
- saute a part of the mushrooms until soft
- set those aside
- saute the rest and the onion
- add the wine and any seasonings
- add the starch to the broth and add it to the sauce
- add the reserved mushrooms and serve
