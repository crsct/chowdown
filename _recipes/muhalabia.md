---

layout: recipe
titel: "Muhalabia - Maroccan Pudding"

tags:
- desserts
- maroccan

ingredients:
- 500 ml milk
- 200 ml cream
- 3 tbps sugar
- 40 g starch
- 1 tsp cardamon
- 1 tsp orangeblossom water
- some almond shards

directions:
- Combine milk, cream and simmer
- let it boil while stirring adding a starch slurry
- add orange or rosewater
- let it cool
- garnish with almond shards
